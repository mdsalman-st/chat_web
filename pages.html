Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the secret chat vault? ▼

Type your secret unlock code (set during signup) into the dashboard's search bar. The encrypted vault opens instantly. Tab switches or locking your device will wipe it clean.

Is my password secure? ▼

Yes. Every password is hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds) + unique salt + application pepper. Even we cannot recover it.

Can I switch my role later? ▼

Absolutely. Head to Settings → Profile and you can re-configure your workspace anytime.

Where is my data stored? ▼

Everything stays on your device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Is MAJID free? ▼

Yes, the core workspace is free forever. Premium features will be optional.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 November 2026  |  Last Revised: 27 November 2026

MAJID ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of every student, teacher, and administrator who uses our platform. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, how we safeguard it, and what rights you have over your data. By using MAJID, you consent to the practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you register, we collect your full name, email address, mobile number, board/class selection, and chosen medium of instruction. This information is necessary to create and authenticate your account.

1.2 Academic & Test Data

We store your enrolled subjects, test responses, scores, AI-generated evaluations, study plans, notes, tasks, calendar events, and answer sheet uploads. This data powers your personalised learning experience, analytics, and progress tracking.

1.3 Device & Usage Data

We automatically collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, screen resolution, IP address, and pages visited. This data is used solely for security monitoring, rate-limiting, and improving platform stability.

1.4 Uploaded Content

Handwritten answer sheets and documents you upload are stored securely on Cloudinary (a third-party cloud storage provider) and are associated with your account for the purpose of AI-based evaluation.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication & Security — To verify your identity, prevent unauthorised access, and protect against fraud.
  • Personalised Learning — To generate AI-powered study plans, recommend practice tests, and provide performance analytics tailored to your board, class, and subject selections.
  • AI Evaluation — To process your handwritten answer sheets using Google Gemini AI for automated scoring and feedback.
  • Communication — To send essential notifications such as OTP codes, test results, and platform updates via email.
  • Platform Improvement — To analyse aggregate (non-identifiable) usage patterns and enhance our features and performance.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for advertising or marketing purposes. We share data only with the following service providers, strictly for operational purposes:

  • MongoDB Atlas — Secure cloud database hosting (data encrypted at rest and in transit).
  • Cloudinary — Secure cloud storage for uploaded answer sheets and documents.
  • Google Gemini API — AI-powered question generation and answer sheet evaluation (no personal data is sent; only academic content).
  • Brevo (Sendinblue) — Transactional email delivery for OTPs and notifications.
  • Render — Application hosting provider.
  • Vercel — Frontend hosting provider.

We may also disclose your information if required by Indian law, court order, or government authority.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with unique salt and pepper — we never store plain-text passwords.
  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • API endpoints are protected by rate limiting, CORS whitelisting, Helmet security headers, and NoSQL injection sanitisation.
  • JWT authentication tokens are used for session management with automatic expiry.
  • Admin and Super Admin accounts have additional OTP-based verification for sensitive operations.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and academic data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (profile, test records, uploaded documents) will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregate analytics may be retained indefinitely for research and improvement purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — View all personal data we hold about you, available through your account Settings page.
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate personal information at any time.
  • Deletion — Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Data Portability — Export your test results and academic records.
  • Withdraw Consent — Stop using the platform at any time; your continued use constitutes consent.

7. Children's Privacy

MAJID is designed for students aged 14 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete that data. Parents or guardians who believe their child has submitted information may contact us at the email below.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

MAJID uses browser localStorage for essential functionality only (authentication tokens, theme preference, session data). We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics trackers beyond basic Google Tag Manager for aggregate traffic insights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Revised" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of MAJID after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Bihar, India.
