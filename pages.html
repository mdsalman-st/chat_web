Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the secret chat vault? ▼

Type your secret unlock code (set during signup) into the dashboard's search bar. The encrypted vault opens instantly. Tab switches or locking your device will wipe it clean.

Is my password secure? ▼

Yes. Every password is hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds) + unique salt + application pepper. Even we cannot recover it.

Can I switch my role later? ▼

Absolutely. Head to Settings → Profile and you can re-configure your workspace anytime.

Where is my data stored? ▼

Everything stays on your device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Is Nexus free? ▼

Yes, the core workspace is free forever. Premium features will be optional.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

Almost nothing. Your account data (name, email, phone) and preferences stay in your browser's local storage. We don't have a server that stores your personal information.

2. How We Use It

Your data is used only to personalize your workspace. We don't sell, share, or rent your information to anyone.

3. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt + salt + pepper before storage. The secret chat vault is session-bound and volatile — nothing is persisted.

4. Cookies

We use minimal localStorage only for essential functionality. No third-party tracking cookies.

5. Your Rights

  • Access your data anytime from Settings
  • Export or delete your data with one click
  • Opt out of any notifications

6. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@nexus.app.
